# Method Validation Report — Troponin I Ultra

- **Study site:** BM-2026-01 Central Laboratory
- **Assay:** ADVIA Centaur Troponin I Ultra (Siemens Healthcare Diagnostics)
- **Platform:** ADVIA Centaur XP
- **Validation standard:** CLSI EP05, EP06, EP07, EP17
- **Report version:** 1.0 · **Date:** 2026-07-30

## 1. Scope

Validation of the Troponin I Ultra assay at the registry's central laboratory, establishing
the analytical limits and the local upper reference limit used by this study.

## 2. Limit of blank, detection and quantitation

- **LoB (limit of blank):** not separately reported
- **LoD (limit of detection):** **0.006 ng/mL**
- **LoQ (limit of quantitation, 10 % CV):** **0.03 ng/mL**
- **Upper limit of quantitation:** **50 ng/mL**
- **Measuring range:** 0.006 – 50 ng/mL

Values between LoD and LoQ are **detected but not quantifiable**.

## 3. Precision

- Repeatability and within-laboratory precision evaluated across the measuring range.
- At the LLoQ (0.03 ng/mL) the observed **CV ≈ 10 %**.

## 4. Linearity

- Linearity accepted over the measuring range per EP06.

## 5. Interference and cross-reactivity

Evaluated per EP07; no significant interference below the stated thresholds from:

- bilirubin, haemoglobin, triglycerides
- heparin
- heterophile antibodies and HAMA
- cTnI-specific autoantibodies

Known physiological confounders (documented for interpretation, not analytical artefacts):

- elevated concentrations with advanced age
- elevated concentrations with reduced renal function

## 6. Reference interval (upper reference limit)

- The **99th-percentile upper reference limit is 0.04 ng/mL**.
- Derived from **5000 population-based subjects in the Gutenberg Heart Study**.
- The 10 % CV concentration (0.03 ng/mL) lies **below** the 99th percentile, so only the
  99th percentile is used as the decision limit.

Interpretation caveats to carry forward:

- The reference limit derives from the named reference population; a different age or sex
  distribution shifts the false-positive rate.
- Because the 10 % CV (0.03 ng/mL) sits just below the decision limit (0.04 ng/mL), values
  near the limit carry roughly 10 % analytical variability — a single marginal value is not
  definitive.

## 7. Conclusion

The Troponin I Ultra assay is fit for purpose for the registry at:
LoD 0.006 ng/mL · LLoQ 0.03 ng/mL · ULoQ 50 ng/mL · 99th-percentile URL 0.04 ng/mL.
