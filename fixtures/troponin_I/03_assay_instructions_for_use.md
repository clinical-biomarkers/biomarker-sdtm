# Instructions for Use (IFU) — ADVIA Centaur Troponin I Ultra

> Manufacturer's package insert, transcribed for the registry. This is the authoritative
> source for the assay name, platform, method, specimen requirements and analytical limits.

## 1. Intended use

The ADVIA Centaur Troponin I Ultra assay is an in-vitro diagnostic immunoassay for the
quantitative determination of cardiac troponin I in human serum or plasma, as an aid in the
diagnosis of acute myocardial infarction.

## 2. Summary and explanation

Troponin I is the inhibitory subunit of the troponin complex that regulates calcium-mediated
contraction of striated muscle. Cardiac troponin I is released into the circulation following
myocyte injury; an increase above the reference limit is the diagnostic finding.

## 3. Principle of the procedure

Two-site (sandwich) chemiluminometric immunoassay. Paramagnetic particles coated with
monoclonal anti-troponin I antibody capture troponin I; a second, acridinium-ester-labelled
monoclonal antibody forms the sandwich; light emission is proportional to the troponin I
concentration.

- **Method (analytical technique):** immunoassay (chemiluminometric)

## 4. Reagents and materials

- Reagent pack: coated paramagnetic particles + labelled antibody; store 2–8 °C.
- Calibrator set (multi-point); lot-specific.
- Quality-control materials at ≥ 2 concentration levels.

## 5. Platform

- **Instrument / platform:** ADVIA Centaur XP
- **Manufacturer:** Siemens Healthcare Diagnostics
- **Assay name (trade name):** Troponin I Ultra (TnI-Ultra)
- **LOINC code:** 10839-9 (Troponin I.cardiac [Mass/volume] in Serum or Plasma)

## 6. Standardization / traceability

There is **no certified primary reference material** for cardiac troponin I, and
standardization across manufacturers is considered unlikely, because monoclonal antibodies to
different cTnI epitopes recognise different circulating modifications. An IFCC working group
pursues harmonisation via a pooled serum-based secondary reference material. Results are
traceable only to the manufacturer's own calibrator lineage.

## 7. Specimen collection and handling (summary)

- **Specimen:** serum or plasma (registry uses **serum**).
- Collect per the laboratory manual; separate within the stated window.
- **Stability:** refer to `05_laboratory_manual_excerpt.md` for the registry's handling rules.

## 8. Measuring range and analytical performance

- **Limit of Detection (LoD):** 0.006 ng/mL
- **Lower Limit of Quantitation (LLoQ):** 0.03 ng/mL (concentration at 10 % CV)
- **Upper Limit of Quantitation (ULoQ):** 50 ng/mL
- **Measuring range:** 0.006 – 50 ng/mL

Precision (representative, from the manufacturer's data):

- at 0.03 ng/mL: CV ≈ 10 %
- imprecision improves at higher concentrations

## 9. Known interferences

- proteolytic degradation of the cTnI molecule
- phosphorylation of cTnI
- complexing of cTnI with cardiac troponin C
- heparin
- heterophile antibodies and human anti-mouse antibodies (HAMA)
- cTnI-specific autoantibodies

(Interference thresholds and acceptance limits are given in the manufacturer's full IFU and
are reaffirmed in `04_method_validation_report.md`.)

## 10. Reporting

Report in **ng/mL**, 3 decimal places. Report results below the LLoQ as "<LLoQ".
